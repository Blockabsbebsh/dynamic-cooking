# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release. Breath of the Wild style cooking with vanilla ingredients, for Minecraft 26.3 with Fabric.

- **Cooking pot:** put it over a campfire, furnace, fire, magma or lava, add up to five ingredients and cook. The dish is named after what went in, like Beef and Potato Stew or Poppy Tea. A mix that fits no dish becomes Dubious Mush.
- **17 dishes.** In the pot: cake, pie, cookies, soup, stew, omelette, roast, porridge, tea and warm milk. On a crafting table, in any shape: sandwich, skewer, kelp roll, salad, juice, jelly and ice cream.
- **Every vanilla food is an ingredient,** plus plants, flowers, leaves, eggs and seasonings. Ingredients keep their effects, and flowers give their suspicious stew effects.
- **Dishes keep cooking on the heat:** soup simmers into stew, and anything left too long burns into Dubious Mush. Look at the pot to see what's in it and how long is left.
- **Bowls, bottles and buckets:** take dishes out of the pot in any of them. Thick dishes don't fit in a bottle, and a bucket holds three servings.
- **Every dish is drawn in its ingredients' colours,** in the item and in the pot.
- **Cakes are placed and eaten a slice at a time,** or cut into slices with a sword.
- **Cooking Guide:** a book and a bowl make an in-game guide to every step and dish.
- **Data driven:** ingredients and dish types are JSON, so data packs can add their own.
